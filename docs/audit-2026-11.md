# PixelHyd audit — November 2026

Audit of branch `feature/residential-discovery` at `62ab0ef`, read-only, 2026-11-25. Approved work and its
status were added on 2026-11-26. This document is the single record of the audit; the Atlas design is in
[atlas-plan.md](atlas-plan.md).

Evidence labels: **[verified]** checked in code, data or the running app; **[measured]** a number observed
on this machine; **[not tested]** not checked in this audit.

## 1. Executive assessment

PixelHyd is in good shape for a working map. Its geographic core is sound: one exact projection (UTM 44N to
2:1 dimetric, tested against pyproj) and seamless chunked rendering. The data rules separate verified,
estimated and unknown values. Home search already handles societies and PGs in one model.

The main weaknesses are accuracy gaps and ageing data, not the architecture:

- The airport is drawn without runways.
- The company layer cannot represent a company with several sites.
- Rent and PG figures age with no warning.
- One render error in a side panel can blank the whole app.

## 3. Strengths to keep

- One projection for the whole city, shared exactly by Python and TypeScript.
- `test_seams.py`: a chunked render is pixel-identical to a whole render.
- Building heights carry a source and a confidence.
- Company sites need a *named* footprint, never proximity alone.
- Home-search verdicts (match / likely / unknown / no) give a stated reason and a source per check; unknown
  never counts as a pass.
- PGs are part of the same search, map, filters, commute and comparison (no separate PG product).
- Operators' terms are respected: unreadable pages are marked "(not read)".

## 4. Confirmed critical issues

## 13. Acceptance criteria (Phase A)

- **G1**: runways, taxiways and aprons come from OSM `aeroway`. Widths come from OSM `width`, or from an
  ICAO minimum recorded as `estimated_icao_minimum`. Only the airport tiles are re-prepared and re-rendered;
  no other raw data is re-downloaded. Seams stay identical. The dusk look grades the new colours, with no
  street life on airside paving.
- **R1**: a benchmark older than 12 months says how old it is and a fit stays at most "likely". After 24
  months, or when undated, it cannot decide a budget ("unknown").
- **R2**: the PG card shows the check date and its age. Page facts become "likely" after 90 days and
  "unknown" after 365 days. Gender read from a listing name and closures are not affected.
- **U1**: a failing card or comparison is replaced by a closable notice; the map keeps working; the notice
  clears when the panel shows something else.
- **T1**: the cause is confirmed (timeout under load) and the test has a budget that fits a busy laptop.
- All existing suites, the type check and lint still pass.
